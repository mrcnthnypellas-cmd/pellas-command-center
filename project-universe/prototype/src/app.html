About the display scale

Real distances would make the planets invisible specks, so the view compresses distance (by the square root of AU) and size. The Moon is drawn farther from Earth than reality. All numbers in the info panel are the real, approximate values.

Data sources

NASA Goddard Planetary Fact Sheet; E. M. Standish, JPL “Keplerian Elements for Approximate Positions of the Major Planets”. Planet surfaces are procedurally generated artwork, not real maps.
